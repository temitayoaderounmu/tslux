FAQ

Quick answers before you book

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date. Popular weekends fill up quickly, especially in spring and summer.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A deposit is required to hold your date. The final quote confirms the current amount.

Do you travel outside DFW?

Yes, in many cases. Share your event location and we’ll confirm travel availability and fees.

How long does setup take?

Most balloon installs take about 1–2 hours, depending on size, access, and customization.

Can I choose my colors?

Absolutely. We’ll match your event theme and help you choose colors that look clean in photos.

Do you handle breakdown?

Yes. Breakdown or removal can be requested when you book, depending on the setup.
